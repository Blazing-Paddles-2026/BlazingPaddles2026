Welcome to the Blazing Paddles 2026 Dashboard.

This site is for committee members and volunteers. It is the working place for planning, sponsor outreach, raffle donations, flyers, event operations, owner assignments, and follow-up.

Start on the dashboard home page. Each card opens a different working area. If you are not sure where to go, start there.

Use the Sponsor Playbook before reaching out to a business. It now includes the sponsorship levels, the Sponsor Outreach Workflow, and a quick match guide to help volunteers decide whether a business is a better fit for cash sponsorship, raffle prizes, or in-kind support.

Use Sponsor Targets for the business list. Before contacting anyone, check the Donation Tracker first. If someone already owns that lead, do not contact the business without checking with that person.

Use the Raffle Strategy page for prize ideas and businesses to contact. The goal is to ask for useful prizes and complete packages, not random items that are hard to manage.

Use the Outreach Scripts page when you need wording for an email, phone call, text message, follow-up, sponsor yes response, raffle prize pickup, or polite decline response. Pick the script that matches the situation, personalize it, and keep the next step clear.

Use the Flyers page to open or download the three current flyer files: the Player Flyer, Light Up The Crew, and Join The Crew. These are the files to send when someone asks for event information.

The Donation Tracker is the most important working tool on the site. There are three rules. First, check before you contact. Second, claim the lead by putting your name in the Owner column. Third, log every answer, whether it is yes, no, maybe, or follow up later.

Use one row per business or donor. Keep the status current. Add the next follow-up date. If something is received, mark it received. If the thank-you has been sent, mark it thanked.

Use the Contacts and Owners page when you are not sure who should handle a question. It has role slots for the event lead, sponsor lead, raffle lead, tracker lead, flyers and website lead, volunteer lead, operations lead, recognition lead, and finance or payment lead.

Use the Planning Timeline to see what needs to happen between now and tournament day.

Use Event Operations and the Day-of Checklist as the event gets closer. Those pages cover setup, volunteer stations, check-in, raffle table, signage, court flow, awards, and cleanup.

Use the Recognition Plan to make sure sponsors and donors are thanked properly after the event.

Use KPIs during committee check-ins to see where things stand.

The dashboard only works if everyone uses the same system. Check the tracker before reaching out, keep your rows updated, use the owner page when you need direction, and send any missing information to the web lead so the dashboard stays current.
