Blazing Paddles 2026

Sponsorship levels and ask strategy

Use the tier that matches the business. Confirm logo, payment deadline, and recognition details before promising placement.

Chief · $5,000

Best for one presenting sponsor that wants maximum visibility across branding, social posts, event program, shirt front, emcee mentions, and giveaway materials.

Lieutenant · $2,500

Best for strong local businesses that want signage, printed material placement, two social mentions, emcee recognition, program placement, thank-you communication, and shirt-back listing.

Firefighter · $1,000

Best for smaller businesses that want event website/signage placement, social recognition during the event, and emcee recognition.
