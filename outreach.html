Blazing Paddles 2026

Use clear, direct asks

Use these exactly as written or make them sound like you. The goal is simple: ask clearly, make it easy to say no, and send Diedra the details when someone says yes.

Keep it specificName the event, the ask, and the next step.
Do not over-explainShort messages are easier for businesses to answer.
Do not over-trackNo one needs to log every no. Send details when someone is interested or says yes.

Auction package choices

Give businesses a clear way to help

Use these package ideas to make the ask specific. Volunteers do not need to ask one business for the whole package. Ask for one piece: a gift card, service, item, experience, or certificate that fits the theme.

Backyard Pitmaster

Good fits: grill or smoker, meat market gift card, BBQ restaurant gift card, rubs, sauces, cooler, cutting board, grill tools, outdoor serving pieces.

Date Night

Good fits: restaurant gift cards, dessert, flowers, hotel stay, event tickets, live music, rideshare credit, coffee or brunch gift card.

Family Adventure

Good fits: arcade, bowling, movies, waterpark or activity passes, children’s museum, trampoline park, ice cream, pizza, family restaurant gift cards.

Hill Country Getaway

Good fits: cabin or hotel stay, winery tasting, brewery visit, dinner, coffee, spa service, boutique gift card, local experience.

Luxury Staycation

Good fits: resort or hotel stay, spa service, dinner, brunch, champagne or dessert, salon service, shopping gift card, transportation credit.

Pickleball Enthusiast

Good fits: premium paddles, balls, court bag, private lesson, court time, athletic apparel, shoes, water bottle, recovery or wellness service.

Sports Fan

Good fits: game tickets, team merchandise, sports bar gift card, hotel stay, tailgate supplies, signed item, golf simulator or sports experience.

Texas Tailgate

Good fits: cooler, cornhole boards, tailgating chairs, snacks, grill accessories, team gear, folding table, outdoor speaker, restaurant gift card.

Backyard Oasis

Good fits: fire pit, Adirondack chairs, patio lights, garden center gift card, outdoor rug, planter, outdoor games, pool or patio accessories.

Ultimate Home Chef

Good fits: pizza oven, smoker, cooking class, specialty foods, knives, cutting board, spices, olive oil, cookware, private chef experience.

Best ask“Would you consider donating one item for our [package name] package?”
Best follow-upIf they say yes, send Diedra the contact, item, value, and pickup details.
Best boundaryNo pressure, no repeated asks, and no need to report every decline.

Scripts

Copy, paste, and adjust as needed

Choose the version that fits the contact. Keep the tone normal, respectful, and easy to answer.

Sponsor email

Subject: Blazing Paddles 2026 sponsorship

Hi [Name],

I’m helping with the Round Rock Fire Foundation’s Blazing Paddles Pickleball Tournament on October 10 at Tejas Pickleball Club. We are inviting local businesses to sponsor the event, with proceeds supporting crisis relief, family support, and wellness programs for Round Rock firefighters and their families.

Would [Business] be open to reviewing the sponsorship options this week? No pressure at all. I can send the flyer and sponsorship details if you are open to taking a look.

Thank you,
[Your Name]

Warm contact sponsor ask

Hi [Name], I wanted to reach out personally because I’m helping with the Round Rock Fire Foundation’s Blazing Paddles Pickleball Tournament on October 10.

We are looking for local businesses to sponsor the event. I thought of [Business] because [short reason: local connection, community involvement, past support, or customer base].

Would you be willing to look at the sponsor options? If it is not the right fit, no problem. I appreciate you considering it.

Phone call script

Hi, my name is [Your Name]. I’m helping with the Round Rock Fire Foundation’s Blazing Paddles Pickleball Tournament on October 10 at Tejas Pickleball Club.

We are reaching out to local businesses about sponsorships and silent auction package donations. Is there a good person I should send the information to?

If yes: Thank you. I’ll send a short email with the event flyer and the specific options. What email should I use?

If no: No problem. Thank you for your time.

Voicemail script

Hi [Name], this is [Your Name]. I’m helping with the Round Rock Fire Foundation’s Blazing Paddles Pickleball Tournament on October 10.

We are reaching out to local businesses about sponsorships and silent auction donations that support programs for Round Rock firefighters and their families.

I’ll send a quick email with the event details. If it looks like a fit, I’d be grateful for a reply. If not, no problem at all. Thank you.

Text message version

Hi [Name], this is [Your Name]. I’m helping with the Round Rock Fire Foundation’s Blazing Paddles Pickleball Tournament on Oct. 10. We’re looking for local sponsors and silent auction donations. Would it be okay if I sent you the flyer and details? No pressure if it’s not a fit.

Auction package ask

Hi [Name], I’m helping collect auction packages for the Round Rock Fire Foundation’s Blazing Paddles Pickleball Tournament on October 10.

We are building a [package name] package for the silent auction, and I thought [Business] might be a good fit. Would you consider donating [specific item, gift card, service, or experience]?

We will recognize auction donors through the event materials and thank-you follow-up. No pressure if it is not a fit.

If this is possible, I can coordinate pickup or provide any details you need.

Stop-in script

Hi, I’m [Your Name]. I’m helping with the Round Rock Fire Foundation’s Blazing Paddles Pickleball Tournament.

I wanted to drop off the flyer and ask whether [Business] would consider a sponsorship or a silent auction donation this year. Gift cards, services, experiences, and donated items all help.

No pressure. Who would be the best person for me to leave this with or email?

Sponsor yes next steps

Thank you for agreeing to sponsor Blazing Paddles 2026. We appreciate the support.

To get everything set up, could you please send the best contact name, logo file, preferred business name for recognition, and the sponsorship level or amount confirmed?

We will also confirm payment instructions and any recognition deadlines so everything is included correctly.

Auction package yes next steps

Thank you for donating an auction item for Blazing Paddles 2026. Could you send the item description, estimated value, and the best pickup contact?

If pickup is needed, please let me know the best day and time window. We will make sure the donation is tracked and recognized with the event materials.

What to send Diedra after a yes

Business name: [Business]

Contact person: [Name]

Email / phone: [Contact info]

What they committed: [Sponsorship amount, gift card amount, item, service, or experience]

Invoice needed: [Yes / No / Not sure]

Pickup or delivery details: [What happens next]

Follow-up message

Hi [Name], I’m following up on the Blazing Paddles sponsorship or auction request. We are organizing sponsor recognition and auction inventory now.

If this is something [Business] can support, I can send the details today or answer any questions.

If now is not a good time, no problem. Just let me know so I can update our list.

Polite decline response

Thank you for letting me know. I appreciate you taking the time to consider it.

I’ll update our committee list so we do not continue following up. We appreciate your time and hope to connect again another year.
