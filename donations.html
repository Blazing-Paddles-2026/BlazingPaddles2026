How to use the tracker

What to send after a yes.

Send enough information for Diedra to invoice sponsors, recognize donors, pick up auction items, and thank everyone without having to chase the details later.

Business and contact

Business name, contact person, email, phone number, and the best way to follow up.

Sponsor or cash donation details

Amount committed, sponsorship level if known, invoice contact, preferred recognition name, logo contact, and any deadline.

Auction donation details

Item, gift card, service, or experience; estimated value; restrictions; expiration date; and pickup or delivery needs.

Recognition details

Send the logo, preferred business name, website or social link, and any recognition preference they mention.

What to do with a maybe

If they want more information, email info@roundrockfirefoundation.org with the contact and what they asked for so Diedra can follow up with the right flyer, invoice, or package details.

What not to do

Do not make it complicated, pressure anyone, or feel like you need to report every decline. The important thing is moving real opportunities forward.
