Open scripts and package ideas

What volunteers do not need to track

No one needs to enter every call, every no, or every follow-up attempt into a shared sheet. Keep it easy so people will actually participate.

What Diedra will manage

Diedra will manage invoices, recognition details, pickup coordination, and the master list of confirmed sponsors and auction donations.

ContactBusiness, contact name, email, phone

How to use the tracker

What to send after a yes.

Send enough information for Diedra to invoice, recognize, pick up, and thank the donor without having to chase the details later.

Business and contact

Business name, contact person, email, phone number, and the best way to follow up.

Donation or sponsor details

What they are donating or considering, the estimated value if known, and whether it is a sponsor, auction item, service, or experience.

Pickup or invoice needs

Include whether the item needs to be picked up, whether an invoice is needed, and any deadline or preferred pickup time.

Recognition details

Send the logo, preferred business name, website or social link, and any recognition preference they mention.

What to do with a maybe

If they want more information, send Diedra the contact and what they asked for so she can follow up with the right flyer, invoice, or package details.

What not to do

Do not make it complicated, pressure anyone, or feel like you need to report every decline. The important thing is moving real opportunities forward.
