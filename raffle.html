Choose one auction package

Each team claims one package theme below for the 🏓 Baddle of the Paddle Silent Auction — choose the one your team can realistically build. Every package follows the same simple formula, so you always know what to ask for.

The formula

1 anchor item + 5 supporting donations. The anchor is the headline. The five supports round it out into a package people want to bid on.

Pick what you can build

Claim one theme your team has connections for. One strong, complete package beats three half-finished ones.

Send every yes

When a business says yes, email info@roundrockfirefoundation.org with the details so the item gets tracked and the donor gets recognized.

How to use this page — 4 steps

1. Claim a package. Pick your team in the "Package Team" dropdown on a theme below. This saves for everyone — once a team is chosen, that package is taken.

2. Secure the anchor item. Lock down the headline donation first — everything else builds around it. Check off "Anchor confirmed."

3. Add 5 supports. Gather five supporting donations and check each one off. When all six boxes are checked, the package shows Complete.

4. Send each yes. Email every confirmed yes to info@roundrockfirefoundation.org. If you swap an item, check "Using a substitution" and note what you're swapping in.

What to include in every "yes" email

Send to info@roundrockfirefoundation.org: donor name; contact info; item or service; estimated value; restrictions or expiration; and pickup or delivery details.

Package themes — claim one

Each card lists the target value, the anchor item, supporting donations to gather, and businesses worth asking. Pick your team to claim a package, check off items as they're confirmed, and note any swaps. Everything saves live for the whole committee.
