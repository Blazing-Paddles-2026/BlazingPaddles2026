Ask for one specific thing

“Would you donate a $150 gift card?” is easier to answer than “Can you donate something?”

Bundle small donations

Several smaller gift cards can become one stronger auction package.

Auction ask script

Hi [Name], I’m helping collect auction items for the Round Rock Fire Foundation’s Blazing Paddles 2026 tournament. Would [Business] consider donating [specific item, gift card, service, or experience]? We will recognize auction donors and make pickup easy.

Follow-up script

Hi [Name], I’m following up on the auction donation request for Blazing Paddles 2026. If [Business] can donate [specific item], I can pick it up this week or send the best delivery details. Thank you for considering it.
