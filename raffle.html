For committee members and volunteers

Raffle prize ideas and businesses to contact

Use this as the volunteer call list. Assign one owner per business, track the ask in the donation tracker, and mark whether the item is requested, committed, received, photographed, and thanked.

Best target prize value

$100–$500 per prize or package. Bundle smaller donations into one stronger raffle item.

Best ask format

Ask for a specific item: “Would you donate a $150 gift card?” works better than “Can you donate something?”

Best volunteer rhythm

Ask, follow up in five business days, confirm pickup, take a photo, and send thank-you wording to the recognition owner.

Volunteer ask script

Hi [Name], I’m helping collect raffle prizes for the Round Rock Fire Foundation’s Blazing Paddles 2026 tournament. We are putting together strong raffle packages for committee fundraising, and I wanted to ask whether [Business] would donate [specific item or gift card amount]. We will track the donation, include your business in our thank-you list, and make pickup easy.

Follow-up script

Hi [Name], I’m following up on the raffle prize request for Blazing Paddles 2026. If [Business] can donate [specific item], I can pick it up this week or send the best delivery details. Thank you for considering it.
