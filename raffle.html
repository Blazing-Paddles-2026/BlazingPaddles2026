Blazing Paddles 2026

Build a raffle people talk about

Aim for fewer, better prizes. Secure prizes before ticket sales and confirm raffle rules with the foundation treasurer before publishing.

High-end categories

Weekend getaway, private chef or dining package, spa day, guided fishing or hunting trip, golf foursome, premium pickleball package, sports tickets, jewelry, home services, tech bundle, wine or bourbon basket, and family entertainment package.

Best asks

Ask for a complete experience instead of one item. Example: dinner, hotel, and activity packaged as one featured prize.

Compliance note

No cash, alcohol, or firearms as raffle prizes unless the foundation has confirmed legal requirements. Raffle ticket purchases are generally not tax deductible.
