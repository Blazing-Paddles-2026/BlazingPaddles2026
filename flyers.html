How to Use These

  • Sponsor outreach: Attach the Sponsor Flyer to every cold email and follow-up. Print 25 copies to leave with prospects in person.
  • Player recruiting: Share the Play Flyer in pickleball clubs, gyms, community boards, and social posts. $55/player via FluidPB.
  • Volunteer recruiting: Post the Volunteer Flyer in firehouses, employee break rooms, and community spaces. QR code takes them straight to volunteer sign-up.
  • Social posts: Crop the top third of any flyer for an Instagram square. The tagline + logo combo works on its own.
  • Don't recreate: These are the only approved 2026 flyers. If you need a different size or layout, ask Diedra rather than editing.
