Current committee workflow

Volunteers should use the Outreach Scripts page, pick an auction package theme, and contact three businesses or personal contacts by the weekly deadline. If a business says yes or wants more information, send the details to Diedra.

No one needs to set up a spreadsheet, paste a webhook, or log every call. Keep the process simple so people will actually use it.

What to send to info@roundrockfirefoundation.org after a yes

  • Business: Business name and contact person
  • Contact: Email or phone number
  • Commitment: What they are donating or considering
  • Details: Estimated value, pickup needs, invoice needs, logo, and recognition preferences
