Compliance Reminders

Read this every week.

  • Texas Charitable Raffle Enabling Act: max 4 raffles per year, prize cap $75,000, no cash, no alcohol, no firearms — and prizes must be in hand before tickets sell.
  • IRS rules: raffle ticket purchases are not tax deductible. Sponsorships and direct donations to a 501(c)(3) generally are — always defer to the treasurer for receipt language.
  • Form W-2G + 25% withholding may apply when net winnings exceed $600 and 300× ticket cost. Coordinate with treasurer.
