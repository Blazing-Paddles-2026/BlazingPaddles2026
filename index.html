Start Here

This is the simple plan for volunteers.

Use this section when someone asks, “What do you need me to do?” Keep the work clear, practical, and easy to finish.

This week

  • Pick one auction package theme.
  • Contact three businesses or personal contacts by next Friday.
  • Use the Outreach Scripts page if you need wording.
  • Send Diedra the details when someone says yes or wants more information.
  • Do not track every no or every unanswered message.

Sponsorship help

  • Ask whether a business would consider sponsoring Blazing Paddles.
  • Share the sponsor flyer if they want details.
  • Make warm introductions when you know the owner or manager.
  • Send Diedra the contact if they need an invoice or sponsor packet.

Auction package help

  • Choose a package theme before asking.
  • Ask for one item, gift card, service, or experience that fits the theme.
  • Focus on strong packages instead of random donations.
  • Send Diedra the donor, item, value, and pickup details when confirmed.

Player registration help

  • Share the player flyer with friends, coworkers, neighbors, and pickleball contacts.
  • Post in local pickleball groups when allowed.
  • Invite people to register early so the tournament builds momentum.

Send Diedra after a yes

  • Business name and contact person.
  • Email or phone number.
  • Sponsor amount or donated item.
  • Estimated value, pickup needs, invoice needs, logo, and any deadline.

Do not overcomplicate it

  • No shared spreadsheet required for volunteers.
  • No reporting every decline.
  • No pressure on businesses, firefighters, spouses, or friends.
  • No promises on recognition details unless Diedra confirms them.
