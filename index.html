Sponsor Targets

Start with high-fit local businesses.

Use this as the first call list. Assign an owner before anyone reaches out so businesses do not hear from multiple committee members.

Chief-level prospects

Dell Technologies, Kalahari Resorts Round Rock, H-E-B, Baylor Scott & White Round Rock, St. David’s Round Rock, Round Rock Express, and major construction or development firms with local visibility.

Lieutenant-level prospects

Restaurants, breweries, banks, real estate groups, insurance agencies, car dealerships, urgent care clinics, home builders, law firms, and regional employers in Round Rock, Georgetown, Cedar Park, Hutto, and Pflugerville.

Firefighter-level prospects

Small businesses that want community recognition: gyms, salons, HVAC, plumbing, roofing, landscapers, coffee shops, boutiques, family entertainment, and pickleball-related businesses.

Raffle Strategy

Build a raffle people talk about.

Aim for fewer, better prizes. Give volunteers a specific business, a specific ask, and a clear pickup or delivery plan.

High-end prize categories

Weekend getaway, private chef or dining package, spa day, guided fishing or hunting trip, golf foursome, premium pickleball package, sports tickets, jewelry, home services, tech bundle, wine or bourbon basket, and family entertainment package.

Best asks

Ask for a complete experience instead of one item. Example: dinner, hotel, and activity packaged as one featured prize. It sounds larger and gives more sponsors visibility.

Businesses to start with

Kalahari Resorts, Round Rock Express, Tejas Pickleball Club, The Picklr Round Rock, City Post Chophouse, 2020 Market, Viva Day Spa, Hand & Stone Round Rock, Hometown Gift & Decor, local dentists, orthodontists, HVAC companies, landscapers, and car detailers.

Outreach Scripts

Use clear, direct asks.

Keep messages short. Say what the event is, who it benefits, what you are asking for, and the deadline for artwork or prize pickup.

Sponsor email

Subject: Blazing Paddles 2026 sponsorship

Hi [Name], I’m helping with the Round Rock Fire Foundation’s Blazing Paddles Pickleball Tournament on October 10 at Tejas Pickleball Club. We are inviting local businesses to sponsor the event, with proceeds supporting crisis relief, family support, and wellness programs for Round Rock firefighters and their families. Would you be open to reviewing the sponsorship options this week?

Raffle prize ask

Hi [Name], we are collecting high-quality raffle prizes for Blazing Paddles 2026, benefiting the Round Rock Fire Foundation. Would [Business] consider donating [specific item or experience]? We will recognize raffle donors at the event and in committee thank-you materials.

Follow-up

Hi [Name], I’m following up on the Blazing Paddles sponsorship/prize request. We are finalizing sponsor recognition and raffle inventory now. If this is something [Business] can support, I can send the details and deadline today.

Event Operations

Make tournament day easy to run.

Volunteer stations

Check-in, court flow, raffle table, sponsor table, food and water, awards, photography, and cleanup.

Physical setup

Directional signs, sponsor banners, raffle display, prize claim area, first aid, shade, water, trash, and backup supplies.

Run of show

Arrival, check-in, welcome, play blocks, raffle pushes, sponsor recognition, awards, final raffle draw, and cleanup.

Day-of Checklist

Use this before anyone leaves home.

  • 1

    Print and pack

    Brackets, sponsor list, raffle item list, volunteer assignments, emcee notes, tape, markers, zip ties, pens, clipboards, and cashless payment signs.

  • 2

    Set up sponsor recognition

    Place banners and signs before players arrive. Confirm sponsor names with the emcee sheet.

  • 3

    Secure raffle items

    Set up prize display, item numbers, winner claim process, and one person responsible for prize custody.

  • 4

    Close out

    Take photos, collect signs, confirm payments, record winners, thank volunteers, and list every sponsor follow-up due next week.

Recognition Plan

Thank sponsors quickly and specifically.

Before event

Confirm logo, spelling, sponsor level, and social media preferences. Send what their sponsorship supports.

During event

Use emcee recognition, signage, sponsor table visibility, and photos of sponsor assets in place.

After event

Send a thank-you within 72 hours with photos, amount raised, beneficiary language, and an invitation to support next year.
