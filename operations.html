Operations

Event operations.

Everything that has to be on-site and ready by 6:30 AM on tournament day. Vendors, signage, food, music, courts, registration, auction table, awards. Owners get assigned and locked by T-5 weeks.

Nine operational lanes

Volunteer Crew

Target 30 volunteers across 3 shifts: early setup (6:30 AM), mid-day operations (10 AM), tear-down (4 PM). Roles: check-in, court monitors, auction table, food, kids zone, photographers, setup/tear-down. Recruitment push begins T-6.

Check-in & Brackets

Two check-in tables. Pre-printed roster sorted alphabetically. Bracket boards printed and posted. Late-add backup table. Wristbands or hand stamps for player vs. spectator distinction.

Auction Table
